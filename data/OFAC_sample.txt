Release Date 03/24/2023
Press Release Link Treasury Sanctions Jet Fuel Suppliers and Military Cronies in Burma Prior to Armed Forces Day
Treasury Targets Belarusian State-Owned Enterprises, Government Officials, and Lukashenka’s Aircraft
Recent Actions Body
The Department of the Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) is amending the Belarus Sanctions Regulations and reissuing them in their entirety. This regulatory amendment is currently available for public inspection with the Federal Register and will take effect upon publication in the Federal Register on March 27, 2023. OFAC is also publishing an alert, "Sanctions Risks Associated with Provision of Jet Fuel to the Burmese Military." (this content is no longer available)
